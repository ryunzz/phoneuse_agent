Hold Option+Space while speaking, then release to fill the prompt. Or click Record voice prompt, then Stop recording. Press Enter to preview. Whisper transcribes locally on this Mac. Review the transcript and interpreted command before confirming. Maximum recording: 30 seconds.






Commands
status · temperatures · home · home x y · jog x 5 · jog z -1 · heat nozzle 200 · heat bed 60 · cooldown · pause · resume · cancel


Jog is relative, at most 10 mm per command. Cancel requests print cancellation; it is not a hardware emergency stop. Use the printer’s physical controls for an emergency.
